# Changelog

*This documents all notable changes to the Visual Pinball Engine and its dependent projects.*

## Unreleased

Built with Unity 6.5

### Added

- Mechanical sound components for flipper attacks/reflips/releases, named mechanism events, successful kicker releases, cabinet input and passive solenoids, with per-owner sound cooldowns and packaged sound-bank references. Named-event loops resume immediately when their mechanism becomes active again.

- Guided rubbers: a rubber wraps the slots of the Rubber Guides on its posts, is created around the selected guides, and refits itself whenever a guide moves or a slot changes, with a roundness for its free spans and a rest length ([Documentation](https://docs.visualpinball.org/creators-guide/manual/mechanisms/rubbers.html)).
- Rubber slingshots: a slingshot on one free span of a guided rubber, added from the rubber's scene handles or a wizard, with a generated slingshot wall that kicks the ball and closes the switch, a self-wired or game-logic-driven coil, an arm swung by a Motion Controller set up in one click, and a deformation renderer that pushes the cord out with the arm ([Documentation](https://docs.visualpinball.org/creators-guide/manual/mechanisms/slingshots.html)).
- Insert presets: lamp presets (incandescent and LED, with bulb colour, fade times and an output factor), color presets (tint, cavity, lamp emission and the cover's tint and haze) and look presets (Clear Plastic, Colored Plastic), each owning its values. Materials link to a preset and follow it, and the fields a linked color preset owns are locked in the material.
- Every insert shape in the library is a prefab variant that owns its bake. A library insert whose geometry changes is forked into the project with *Edit in project*, for that insert alone or for every insert of the same prefab, material and shape in the scene, keeping the library's cover material.
- A stippled insert pattern: a grid of bumps over the raised floor, set by their spacing, size and height.
- A Mirror option for the flash insert shape.
- The hybrid insert material's Clarity, Cavity Color, Cavity Spread and Cavity Contrast: a clear insert shows the lamp board under it, lit by room light, lamp or no lamp.
- Room Return for clear inserts: room light that comes in through the top, reflects in the grooves and leaves through the top again, followed through the groove, normalised by the sky's brightness and lit by whatever lights the insert. Room Blur and Room Return Exposure soften and scale it.
- Flecks on the hybrid insert material: the nicks along a moulded rib's ridges, dashes that glint with the lights, with a Fleck Size.
- Mottle clouds the unlit insert plastic, blotches lighter and darker than the rest, set by Mottle Amount and Mottle Size.
- Surface grain is drawn in the hybrid insert material instead of baked, as fine at any zoom.
- Insert bakes write the optical maps at a fraction of the normal map's size, cut seams along creases steeper than 15 degrees for sharp facets, go down to 256 pixels, and fall back to Blender's packer when UVPackMaster crashes.
- Packaged inserts keep their GLB meshes, shader materials, lamps and cover depth, with their authoring data in an editor sidecar.
- Toolbox buttons and thumbnail camera presets for inserts and wire rails.
- Assets dropped on the playfield go into or beside the selected object, depending on whether it is a prefab, with a label saying where.
- Light groups pick which lamp of a multi-bulb light they drive, in a reorderable list.
- Lamp Manager has a table lighting row: the kind of bulb the whole table is lit by, and a table-wide insert brightness packed with the table as the player's default.
- Device pickers accept a GameObject dropped on them; an insert dropped into a light group, for example, takes its Insert Light.
- Asset Browser: copy and paste of asset metadata, and previews lit and exposed like a table instead of Unity's default preview.
- Material variation tiles in the Asset Browser can be dragged onto the playfield, previewing and placing the prefab with the displayed material combination.
- Search boxes in Switch, Coil and Lamp Manager filter rows by ID or description, ignoring case. Manager toolbars scroll horizontally with the mouse wheel when their controls exceed the window width, without a visible scrollbar.
- Prefabs dragged from the Asset Browser or Project window preview under the cursor on the playfield and drop at their mounting height, with prefab links and Undo preserved. Playfields need no Unity collider for placement.
- Custom insert outlines from a flat, closed wire or filled mesh, with Inspector validation, shared corner rounding, extrusion, channels, holes, all patterns, and normal/optical baking. Custom source meshes are preserved in the editor sidecar when packaging and restored for editing; the player uses the finished GLB mesh.
- A flash insert's engraved line: a thin trough a little way in from the raised middle's edge, in the starburst's place, chosen under Pattern with its inset, width, depth and rounding. The trough is geometry for the bake alone: while a bake fits, the middle's triangles give way to a plain flat island carrying the maps, the middle's chart having kept its projection through the bake so any triangulation of it carries the map. A geometry change makes the bake stale, and until the next bake the insert's renderer is off and the geometry to be baked is drawn in the scene instead, a yellow see-through body under its wireframe the way a collider preview is drawn, so the line can be tuned without a bake for every step.
- The insert's spill light is a wide spot under the insert pointing up, cheaper than the point light above it that earlier inserts got and aimed; an insert that still has a point light offers to turn it round.
- The hybrid insert material takes up to four virtual bulbs, for a banana with its bulbs in a row: each with a position, a level and a colour of its own, the radius shared, every bulb adding its own halo in its own colour, and each bulb a lamp of its own in Lamp Manager, picked in the Element column by the name the Insert Light gives it, RGB channels included. Every lit bulb lights the body by the inverse square of its distance over the Lamp Spread, bulbs that are off taking nothing away, and Tint With Lamp follows the nearest lit bulb, on the body and the cover alike.
- Insert material templates: the look of an insert's hybrid material and clear cover, everything but the baked maps and the virtual lamps, saved as an asset and put on other inserts from the insert's Material Template section. Clear and Colored ship with the HDRP package; the look of any insert can be saved as another.
- The insert light has the Light's State, Blink Pattern, Blink Interval and fade speeds: the status it starts the game in, how it blinks and how fast it fades when the game drives it; and the insert components carry an insert icon of their own, in the inspector and in Lamp Manager.
- A Start for the rings insert pattern: how far out from the centre the rings begin, leaving a flat disc in the middle.
- A Stipple Offset that moves the grid of stipples across the surface, and a negative stipple height that sinks the stipples into it.
- A Whole Only toggle for the stippled insert pattern: off builds every stipple the surface reaches and cuts it off at the outline, instead of only those that fit on it whole.
- A Roundness setting for the ribbed and rings insert patterns, rolling their ridges and grooves over from sharp creases towards a moulded wave, in the mesh and its normals.
- A gem insert pattern: a flat table with rows of triangular facets zigzagging out from it to the edge, set by the centre, the number of sides, the table's size, the row length, how evenly the facets meet the edge and the height, under Pattern beside the other reliefs.
- A rings insert pattern: concentric ridges and grooves round a centre across the raised floor, set by the centre, the ridge-to-ridge width and the height, under Pattern beside the starburst and the ribs; the bake tells the hybrid material, so the facet variation follows the rings.
- A ribbed insert pattern: parallel ridges and grooves across the raised floor, set by the direction they run, the ridge-to-ridge width and the height, in the starburst's place under Pattern; the bake tells the hybrid material which it found, so the facet variation follows the ribs.
- The hybrid insert material's mottle and grain are three-dimensional noise, the same on any slope: laid over the floor with a share of the height, they were sheared into streaks along one slope of every starburst ridge. The bake's cavity glow is blurred wider, taking out the plateaus the light bouncing between steep pleats left on each facet.
- The insert cover is the top of a slab of plastic as deep as the insert: HDRP's planar refraction bends the view into it by an index of refraction, so the ribs are seen a third nearer than they lie and the top reads as a surface, and the light takes the cover's tint over a tint depth on its way up, deeper the longer its path, so a coloured insert is coloured plastic rather than a lit tray under a pane. The insert writes the slab's thickness into the cover for its own depth.
- The hybrid insert material keeps the bulb in view from every angle: a starburst facet steep enough to trap the view ray inside the plastic, or to throw it out sideways, hands the ray to the straight path instead of losing the bulb, as a narrow-spoked starburst did when seen along its length.
- The hybrid insert material has Lamp Spread: how far from the virtual lamp the lit look reaches before it has fallen to half, so raising Max Brightness brightens the bulb region without flooding the whole body.
- Insert Light has Tint With Lamp: the hue and saturation of the clear cover's haze and of the body turn towards the lamp's while the lamp is lit, in proportion to its intensity and never their brightness, so a coloured lamp is not milked by a grey cover and a lamp that is off leaves both their own colour.
- An insert keeps the material it is given: a bake goes into the material the insert wears when that carries the bake's maps, such as a copy made for another colour, or into a copy of it otherwise, and never puts an older material back. The cover is whatever the renderer's second slot holds. The hybrid material's baked maps and starburst are editable, to be put back after another material's properties were copied over them.
- An arrow insert shape: an arrowhead on two feet with a notch cut up into its base, set by the notch's depth and width and the tilt of the feet, with fillets at the tip, the notch, the inner corners and the feet; it covers the scooped arrow, the arrowhead with a hollow base and the chevron, and fills its width and height exactly.
- A bullet insert shape: a flat base, straight sides up to the shoulders, and flanks that lean in as tangents to a round tip, with fillets on the shoulders and the base corners; the width and height are exact.
- A flash insert shape, the lightning bolt: width, height, tip width, notch depth and step height, with fillets of their own on the tips, the spikes and the notches; it fills its width and height exactly.
- A diamond insert shape, with its own fillets on the side points and on the top and bottom, and a shear that leans it over; the fillets and the shear leave the width and height exactly as set.
- A banana insert shape: a band bent round a circle of a given diameter, with rounded ends, set by its thickness, angle and vertex counts, with a button that centres the starburst on the circle; every option of the other shapes works on it.
- A circle insert shape, drawn as a regular polygon of a chosen diameter and vertex count, with every option of the other shapes.
- The insert's channel is optional: without it the floor is the outline itself, straight down, flat or with the starburst; holes and the bottom bevel need a channel. Depth is now the floor's own depth, with the channel sunk below it.
- The insert's starburst can be switched off, keeping the raised floor flat, and its holes are listed one per line.
- The Insert and Insert Light inspectors are grouped into sections with a tooltip on every control, controls that would do nothing greyed out, and a top view of the insert's outline, raised floor, starburst and holes.
- Insert bakes record the insert they were made for, so a duplicated insert, wherever it ends up, bakes into a folder of its own instead of over the original's.
- Inserts whose bake lives in a read-only package have their bake options locked, with Fork to Project copying the bake into the project as a material variant that keeps following the package's tuning and baking there from then on.
- Insert Light is directly selectable in Lamp Manager and light groups, with ROM on/off, fading, RGB channels, wire events and editor previews driving both the lens and spill light.

- Fine surface grain in the insert normal bake, with adjustable strength and size in VPX units; optical bulb normals and the clear cover remain independent.

- Optional clear insert cover in a second material slot, using any assigned material or automatically detecting and aligning the playfield's normal map across insert position, rotation and scale changes.
- Hybrid insert materials expose Min Brightness for a darker, adjustable unlit body while the bulb and spill remain off at zero intensity.
- Optional hybrid insert optics bake: neutral cavity illumination and sharp optical normals for the HDRP virtual lamp shader, with face contrast, stable optical variation, independent runtime RGB/fading, and insert brightness controls linked to a real spill light.
- Trough ball queues are prefab-aware: one prefab per slot, drained balls return as their own prefab, and the ball manager scales relative to the prefab's radius ([#578](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/578)).
- Simulation trace: a per-tick, per-frame and lock ring buffer written to `Logs/SimulationTrace`, switched on the Simulation Thread component or with `-simulation-trace` ([#577](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/577)).
- Portable content bundles in `.vpe` packages: hash-addressed directory trees with a verified extraction cache, path-traversal protection and per-content user consent ([#574](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/574)).
- Cylindrical surface magnets with configurable damping and a hit switch.

### Changed

- Upgraded from Unity 2022.3 to Unity 6, and later to Unity 6.5 with HDRP 17. New dependencies: Unity Splines and NativeTrees.
- Hybrid optics are on by default for newly lit inserts, and RGB-mapped lamps are treated as game-driven LEDs.
- The VPE editor menus and the insert shaders are under *Pinball* instead of *Visual Pinball* ([#520](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/520)).
- `IGamelogicEngine.Init` is asynchronous, so engines such as MPF connect without blocking (thanks @arthurkehrwald, [#504](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/504)).
- `IApiCoil.OnCoil` takes a float strength instead of a bool.
- Sound assets have a type (synthetic or mechanical), only effects can be spatial, and spam protection moved from the component to the asset ([#521](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/521)).
- Animations are published through generic value emitters, with the animation code in per-item animation components ([#546](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/546)).
- Flipper tricks follow the current nFozzy and rothbauerw implementations.
- Surfaces collide with their render mesh, keeping the side mesh for slingshot colliders.
- Kinematic octree rebuilds are Burst-compiled, and moving kinematic items are kept out of the octree ([#577](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/577)).
- The broad phase queries with the ball's swept bounds instead of the full physics step, cutting the worst tick's hit tests by an order of magnitude ([#577](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/577)).
- The UPM package is signed before publishing.
- Renamed Actuator and Actuator Transform to Motion Controller and Motion Transform in components, APIs, editor menus, and documentation; packages keep their identifiers and coil mappings.
- Removed DOTS in favor of Jobs with Burst ([#459](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/459))
- All geometry is now in world space.
- Removed internal ID in gamelogic engine API ([#408](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/408))
- When importing, meshes are now saved as easily editable `.fbx` files instead of Unity's internal format ([#387](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/387)).
- Revised rubber mesh generation ([#384](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/384)).
- APIs for RGB lamps and Visual Scripting ([#382](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/382)).
- Playfield is now rotated to the correct angle during gameplay ([#370](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/370)).
- Decouple light components from transformation override ([#350](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/350)).
- Refactored drag points. They are nicely separated and typed now.
- Collider debug view is now much faster and intuitive. It's also activated per default when there is no visible mesh.
- Drop and hit targets are now different components.
- Kicker is now a coil device with different coils for different angles/forces.
- Ground truth of data is now the scene, not the imported data anymore ([#302](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/302)).
- Plunger is now a coil device, meaning it can both be pulled back and fired through different inputs.
- Move render pipelines into separate repos ([#259](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/259)).
- Put game-, mesh-, collision- animation data into separate components ([#227](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine/pull/227), [Documentation](https://docs.visualpinball.org/creators-guide/editor/unity-components.html)).

### Removed

- NuGet packages of the core are no longer published.
- The `[MovedFrom]` mappings from the old Actuator and wire rail fixture names; existing tables have been migrated.
- The legacy VPX table patcher and its import option, including `IPatcher`, `PatcherManager`, the render-pipeline material-adapter APIs, and the `applyPatch` import parameters.

### Fixed

- Packaged tables preserve the drop target's visual animation, its emitter and animation curves, and event-driven rotations such as the Terminator 2 cannon.
- Insert Light fades advance once per frame instead of once per lamp event.
- Packages saved before lamp mappings stored their device's type keep their lamp links.
